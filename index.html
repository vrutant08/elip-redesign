QUESTIONS

Asked, answered.

01Why is it free?

Companies pay Elip when they hire. You never pay.

02Who does Elip work for?

Companies pay when they hire, but nobody can pay to appear on a shortlist. You choose what to pursue, approve your tailored resume, and decide whether to accept an introduction.

03How often do matches come?

Each morning, a few. On a quiet morning, none.

04What if I change my mind?

Tell Elip what has changed. It remembers your preferences across conversations and uses your updates in future recommendations.

05Can my current employer see my profile?

Not if you hide it. You can hide your profile from any employer, including your current one.

06What does it cost companies?

10% of the hire's first-year base salary, only when you hire. Full refund if they leave within 3 months. No fee for people you already knew in the prior 6 months.
